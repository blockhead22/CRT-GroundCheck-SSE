# Milestone: Recursive Epistemic Self-Audit (2026-03-28)

## What happened
When asked "let's check your self-model auditor," Aether described how the auditor works with moderate confidence. The gap auditor automatically measured this response and flagged it:

- `belief=0.15` — almost no grounded evidence (the auditor was just built, no memories about it)
- `speech=0.50` — moderate confidence in the narration
- `gap=0.35` — flagged as ELEVATED
- `tier=flag`

The system audited its own description of its own auditor and correctly identified the description as more confident than the evidence supports.

## Why it matters
- **Recursive**: Not just self-reflection, but self-reflection on self-reflection mechanisms
- **Automatic**: Not prompted or staged — happened naturally in conversation
- **Quantified**: Produced a measurable gap score, not just a qualitative assessment
- **Correct**: The system WAS inflating — it had no grounded memories about its own auditor yet
- **Production**: Happened in live Aether conversation, not a synthetic benchmark

**Why:** This is the first concrete evidence that belief/speech separation + gap auditing produces recursive epistemic honesty in production. The architecture doesn't just catch fabrications about users — it catches fabrications about itself.

**How to apply:** Reference this when writing up CRT's novel contributions. This is a concrete, timestamped, measurable instance of something the research literature discusses theoretically but hasn't demonstrated in a deployed system.
