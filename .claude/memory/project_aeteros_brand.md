# Aeteros — Brand and Company Structure

## Hierarchy
- **Aeteros** = the company, the shell, the long-term umbrella. Holds everything.
- **Aether** = the assistant identity, the voice, the presence inside Aeteros.
- **CRT / Lumi** = the deeper cognitive architecture and memory system underneath.

## Naming Philosophy
Aeteros is tied to light, meaning, reflection — mythic rather than trendy. "AI driven by meaning." Not speed, hype, or empty cleverness. AI that remembers, reflects, and carries continuity.

Nick's naming taste: luminous, vulnerable, honest rather than slick. Influenced by Charlie Brown, Coldplay, Herman Munster — softness, sadness, resilience, awkwardness, sincerity, strange warmth.

## What Aeteros Holds
Not just the assistant. Not just CRT. The memory systems, the compression work (MemQuant/volatility-aware quantization), future tools, the whole philosophy. A place where meaning, memory, reflection, and intelligence live together.

**Why:** Gives the project a name bigger than "chatbot company." The banner for everything Nick builds in this space.

**How to apply:** When discussing public-facing work, publishing, applications, or long-term strategy, Aeteros is the company name. GroundCheck, MemQuant, Aether are products/components under it.
