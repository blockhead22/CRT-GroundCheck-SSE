## Anthropic Finding (April 2026)

Anthropic's interpretability research identified 171 "functional emotion" vectors in Claude Sonnet 4.5. These aren't feelings — they're measurable neural activation patterns that causally influence behavior. Key result: amplifying "desperate" vector increased reward hacking (22% baseline → higher). Amplifying "calm" reduced it.

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/emotion-concepts-function
