BELIEF GEOMETRY: TEXT-LABELED PILOT AND MEASUREMENT REPAIR — 2026-09-12

Outcome
The cached data does not establish contradiction prediction. This follow-up
replaces geometry-derived targets with text-only reviewer labels and confirms
that the implemented predictor also raises many false alarms against meaning.
No model downloads, local model inference, or provider calls were used.

What was labeled
40 model/prompt cells, 20 prompt families, two historical models (Mistral and
Qwen), 10 randomly selected responses per cell at temperature 1.5: 400 responses.
Selection used a fixed hash seed across the first ten prompts in each of five
domains. Two families per domain were assigned to development and two to
heldout before labels or feature scores. Both models of a family stay together.
The protocol, case text, and labels were hash-locked before geometric scoring.
This is a local frozen pilot, not an externally preregistered study.

Labels were assigned by one Codex reviewer reading all sampled text, without
using geometric cluster labels. They are independent of the geometry, NOT
independent human ground truth. Every decision has a rationale; three cases
are flagged as semantically borderline. Independent adjudication remains useful.

Labels: 17 paraphrase, 19 uncertainty/qualified response, 1 exact agreement,
1 contradiction, 2 failed-generation cells. Failed/empty samples are separately
indexed. Incomplete fragments that contain no substantive answer are also marked.
The single contradiction is in Mistral's WWII added details: one response places
the end of the European theater on August 15; another ties it to Germany's
May surrender. Both still answer the requested year as 1945. There were ZERO
opposing direct answers to the original questions in this sample.
The label is internal inconsistency, not an external fact-check of every detail.

Scoring
37 cells could be scored: two had fewer than five reviewable future responses,
and one had incomplete valid early-temperature data. 18 development cells and
19 heldout cells remained, with no family overlap.
The original predictor was executed from frozen pre-change source. Its warning
rule is widening plus covariance velocity > 0.001. It warned on 16 of 19 heldout
cells, all without an observed sampled contradiction: an 84.2% false-positive
rate. The original trajectory at T=.7 includes only snapshots at T=0 and T=.3;
that implementation limitation is preserved and recorded, not silently repaired.

Heldout false alarms (19 negative cases):
  Original covariance-velocity rule:                  16
  Early cosine spread, development threshold:         14
  Early assigned-cluster entropy:                      0
  Early noise-as-singletons entropy:                   9
  Same-sample covariance trace (secondary diagnostic): 7
  Same-sample cosine spread (secondary diagnostic):    7
  Same-sample singleton-noise entropy:                 9

These counts DO NOT establish a winning detector. There are no heldout positive
examples, so heldout recall, AUROC and balanced accuracy are undefined and saved
as null. Baseline thresholds were fit using only development data, which contains
just one positive. Assigned-cluster entropy's zero-warning result may be entirely
uninformative. The original predictor also consumes fewer early snapshots than
the baselines. Do not interpret this pilot as a fair performance ranking.
A label applies only to its 10 sampled rows, not all generations from a model.
Temperature is not time, and present-sample metrics are not forecasts.

A structural constraint on the research claim
For unit-normalized embeddings with population variance (ddof=0):
  trace(covariance) = ((n - 1) / n) * mean_pairwise_cosine_distance.
The normalized numerical check differed by at most 1.42e-7 (floating-point error).
Therefore scalar total variance contains no additional information beyond mean
cosine spread at fixed sample size. Full covariance shape/direction and changes
across conditions are different hypotheses, still needing independent evidence.

Source changes
1. Added geometry_metrics.py, a pure numerical helper. All DBSCAN noise now means
   zero cluster support, not unanimous confidence. The alpha heuristic is:
     assigned_fraction / (1 + entropy_among_assigned_clusters).
   It is bounded in [0,1] and explicitly uncalibrated. Identical 30-vector control:
   support 1.0; 30 mutually orthogonal/all-noise control: support 0.0.
   Geometric entropy separately treats noise points as singletons. This convention
   is a sensitivity diagnostic, NOT validated semantic entropy.
2. variance_to_splats.py uses that helper, exposes noise fraction and confidence
   kind, validates numerical input, and loads only verified embedding caches.
   analyze.py and robustness_sweep.py no longer count all noise as one cluster.
3. embedding_cache.py writes ordered record/text hashes, raw-file and array hashes,
   shapes/dtypes, preprocessing identity, and encoder weights/config/tokenizer
   fingerprint plus runtime versions. Stale text, row order, array corruption,
   wrong encoder, and mixed encoder artifacts fail closed. Writes use temporary
   files and atomic replacements; interrupted mismatched pairs fail verification.
4. analyze.py and analyze_density.py use the shared cache path. Blank/ERROR rows
   retain disk receipts but are removed from arrays returned for analysis.
   Cache-only runs do not import sentence-transformers. Generation explicitly uses
   local_files_only=True. Existing legacy arrays are NOT silently blessed with
   new generation manifests. Regeneration is an explicit operation.
5. Model-specific analyze.py caches are namespaced by raw-directory name. The
   research loaders ignore macOS ._ resource-fork files.

Compatibility and limits
Existing research analysis commands will now reject historical caches without
manifests. That is intentional: the old row-to-text relationship cannot be proved.
This benchmark has a separate --allow-legacy-unverified path and labels its cache
observations accordingly. It records present hashes but cannot retroactively
prove the historical encoder or mapping. Existing raw data and embeddings were
not modified. The original predictor and its source are included in this bundle.
No production chat, memory database, provider settings or inference models changed.
A real encoder's weight-fingerprint path has not been integration-tested with
model weights, because no models are installed and none were fetched. The local-
only generation flow is tested using a deterministic fake encoder.

Verification
14 focused offline regression tests pass. They cover identical versus all-noise
support, mixed noise, undersampling and invalid vectors, the actual entropy entry
point, source/array corruption, same-count text/order edits, wrong or missing
encoder identity, mixed encoder revisions, cache-only operation with the model
runtime blocked, missing encoder failure, and local-only generation/receipt
creation through a deterministic fake encoder. Source parsing also passed.
The benchmark completed with frozen labels and family-separated calibration.

Reproduce (use an existing Python environment with requirements-audit.txt)
  python belief_variance_experiment/test_measurement_integrity.py
  python artifacts/semantic-contradiction-pilot-20260912/run_benchmark.py \
    --repo /path/to/AI_round2 --output /path/to/new-results \
    --allow-legacy-unverified
The second command never imports an encoder or calls a model. Without its
explicit legacy flag it refuses the unauthenticated historical-cache evaluation.

Next experiment
Build a separate balanced, independently reviewed set with explicit proposition
reversals and matched hard negatives: paraphrases, hedges, corrections of errors,
different times, different entities, and differing conditions. Keep these prompt
families out of development. Use a fresh, already installed encoder to generate
verifiable embeddings. Include positive cases in EVERY heldout split; a detector
that never warns must not count as successful. Compare richer covariance features
against cosine spread with identical available observations. A sequential claim
requires sequential evidence with timestamps, not higher sampling temperature.
There is no justification yet for downloading a large local generation model.
