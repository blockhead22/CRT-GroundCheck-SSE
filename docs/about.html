Names, purpose, and scope

About Aether

A personal-agent project studying how language models can gain useful continuity without being allowed to invent their own history.

The point is not to build another chat wrapper. The point is to make memory, evidence, model choice, tools, and learning inspectable parts of the system.

About
Aeteros Research · 2026

1. Purpose

Language models are powerful renderers, but their fluent answers do not automatically provide durable identity, reliable personal memory, evidence boundaries, or accountable tool use. Aether tests an architectural response: keep those responsibilities in a governed local layer and let the selected model generate language from what that layer releases.

The practical goal is a personal assistant that can grow more useful over time while remaining correctable, inspectable, and under user control.

2. What the names mean now

Current product

Aether

The local Workbench and governed sidecar being actively used and developed. Aether is not merely an earlier prototype; it is the current proof loop.

Repository and implementation

Aether Core

The codebase containing the substrate, governance mechanisms, sidecar, adapters, tests, and research lineage.

Reusable seam

Aeteros Core

A narrow boundary contract being extracted from product-specific behavior. It currently audits governed completions; it is not yet the broad standalone library older pages described.

Umbrella identity

Aeteros

The project and research identity represented by this site. Aeteros may become a wider product or organization, but this documentation does not present that future as an accomplished company structure.

Historical name

CRT

Cognitive-Reflective Transformer was the earlier framing for memory, contradiction, variance, and reflective control. CRT remains part of the lineage but is not the clearest name for today's full system.

Research framework

CORE

A later synthesis of contradiction, confidence, trust, speech, and governance ideas. Some mechanisms informed Aether; CORE should not be read as a single mandatory pipeline currently wrapping every response.

3. What exists today

  • A local Workbench with Simple and Lab views.
  • A sidecar that governs context release, route policy, tool grants, persistence, and completion receipts.
  • A durable substrate for personal states, slot history, contradictions, dispositions, and evidence-scoped retrieval.
  • Local and hosted model renderers, including Grok, under the same Aether authority boundary.
  • Bounded read-only model-proposable tools with explicit grants and receipts.
  • Review surfaces for memory, reflection, support, and learning candidates.
  • Multi-part answer obligations and completion verification.

See Architecture Today for the live request path and Status & Limits for the current capability matrix.

4. What remains research

The archive investigates contradiction density, variance probing, meaning and epistemic compression, geometric memory, backward influence, structural tension, immune agents, scaffolded reasoning, and related mechanisms.

Research is not discarded when it is not yet integrated. Existing papers and lab outputs are preserved as dated artifacts. They must be replicated or rerun before old measurements are promoted to new product claims.

Most evidence is repository-internal. Some experiments use synthetic fixtures or a single long-running personal corpus. That can be meaningful engineering evidence without being external scientific validation.

5. Claims this project does not make

  • Aether is not described as conscious, sentient, or an AGI.
  • Aeteros is not presented here as an established company or certification body.
  • The system has not been independently validated as a general AI safety solution.
  • The architecture has not been legally assessed or certified under the EU AI Act.
  • Related external research does not automatically validate Aether's implementation.
  • The existence of a module does not mean it runs in Workbench.

Governed memory and receipts may support auditability. Whether a particular deployment meets a legal, safety, or scientific standard depends on its use, evidence, and independent assessment.

6. Why it should exist

Model capability is improving quickly, but capability alone does not decide what a personal system should remember, which source it should trust, when it should admit conflict, who may use a tool, or how an answer can be reviewed later. Those are product and architecture questions around the model.

Aether exists to explore that surrounding layer in a concrete, local, user-controlled system—and to report both what works and what still fails.
