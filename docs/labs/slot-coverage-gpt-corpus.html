The contradiction layer's slot-template path can only fire on turns where extract_fact_slots returns at least one slot. 24,725 of 26,049 turns produced none. The remaining 5.08% is the entire surface area available to the structural detector on this corpus — even if every other gate (canonicalization, entity awareness, polarity) were perfect.

Section 4

What this number means

Two things matter here, and they're easy to conflate.

For live single-turn auto-ingest (your daily Claude Code use), the regex extractor probably works fine because turns are short and conversational, the regexes were tuned for that, and the substrate's existing 180 memories show plausible structure.

For batch ingestion of conversational corpora (this GPT export, anyone else's history, support transcripts), the extractor is unsafe to point at directly. 95% of turns are invisible to it, and the 5% it does fire on contain heavy false-positive contamination from code blocks and abstract prose.

The README's "Does NOT (yet)" section currently says "contradictions go undetected outside that vocabulary." This bench puts a number on it: outside-vocabulary is 95% of real conversational data. The roadmap's Phase C (entity-aware extractor) is the relevant fix.
