System Status & Limits

What is usable now, what is intentionally bounded, and what remains research.

Aether is a working local proof loop, not a finished general-purpose autonomous agent or a validated safety standard.

3. Current limitations

LimitStatusConsequence
External validationNot establishedMost evidence is repository-internal, synthetic, single-user, or based on archived corpora.
Broad autonomous toolingNot grantedNormal renderers do not receive shell, write, patch, policy, or unrestricted memory-write authority.
Perfect passive learningIncompleteRepeated and explicit evidence can affect scoring, but false extraction and confirmation remain active research problems.
General natural-language contradiction detectionIncompleteStructured slot contradictions are stronger than open-ended contradictions across arbitrary prose.
Provider-private reasoning tracesNot exposedPublic progress and verification events are available; hidden chain-of-thought is not treated as a product surface.
Hosted public API or proxyNot a current productThe primary proof loop is local Workbench plus sidecar, not a generally available governed API.
Legal or regulatory complianceNot assessedReceipts may support auditability, but Aether is not presented as certified under the EU AI Act or another regime.
Seamless continuity everywherePartialContinuity structures exist, but automatic transfer across every new thread, device, or product surface is not proven.

4. Research boundary

Wobble, Fidelity Mirror, belief backpropagation, geometric memory, immune agents, structural tension, meaning compression, Mirus, Holden, and related CRT/SSE work remain part of the project's intellectual and experimental lineage.

They should not be removed merely because they are not in the current request path. They should be labeled by evidence level and retested before a page upgrades them to a current production capability.

Existing experiment values are preserved on their dated pages. This documentation update changes their status framing, not their measurements.

5. Claim rules used on this site

  • Current: verified in the active product call path or current source boundary.
  • Implemented: present and tested in current source, but runtime activation may depend on a relaunch or configuration.
  • Bounded: available only through an explicit capability, review, or policy boundary.
  • Research: an internal experiment, prototype, or theoretical mechanism; not automatically a product feature.
  • Historical: useful lineage that no longer describes the current architecture.
  • Proposed: roadmap material, not a capability claim.
