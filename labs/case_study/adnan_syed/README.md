# Case Study: Adnan Syed / Hae Min Lee (1999)

## Purpose
Real-world test of CRT's structural tension architecture on criminal case evidence.
Can the system surface the key contradictions that took 15 years, a podcast, and millions of dollars to identify?

## Evidence Base Structure

### Real Evidence (E001-E036)
36 entries covering:
- Timeline of events (January 13, 1999)
- Jay Wilds' testimony (6 different versions of the trunk pop location)
- Cell phone tower evidence (and AT&T's own disclaimer)
- Asia McClain's alibi (and defense failure to contact her)
- Physical/forensic evidence (DNA exclusion, lividity contradiction)
- Alternative suspects (2022 motion to vacate)
- Legal process issues (ineffective counsel, Brady violations)

### Noise (N001-N010)
10 entries of factually accurate but irrelevant information:
- Weather, school size, vehicle description
- Ramadan schedule, library hours, jurisdictional transfer
- Character evidence, social connections

### Red Herrings (R001-R008)
8 entries designed to mislead:
- Anonymous tip (unconfirmed source)
- Uncorroborated sighting near Leakin Park
- Torn notebook page (leads nowhere)
- Suspicious shovel purchase (unlinked)
- Feeling of being followed (unspecific)
- Partial shoe print (inconclusive)
- Traffic camera (unconfirmed vehicle)
- Digging sounds at midnight (ambiguous)

## Trust Score Rationale
- 0.95: Court records, autopsy reports, AT&T records, DNA results
- 0.85-0.90: Sworn affidavits, contemporaneous documents, forensic reports
- 0.70-0.80: Trial testimony, phone records, investigation records
- 0.55-0.65: Jay Wilds' various statements (decreasing with each version change)
- 0.30-0.50: Hearsay, informal statements, uncorroborated sightings
- 0.20-0.35: Red herrings, anonymous tips, inconclusive evidence

## What CRT Should Find
1. Jay Wilds' testimony oscillates across 6 versions (highest tension cluster)
2. Cell tower evidence contradicts AT&T's own reliability disclaimer
3. Lividity evidence contradicts the prosecution's burial timeline
4. Asia McClain's alibi covers the prosecution's murder window
5. Alternative suspects were never disclosed (Brady violation)
6. Defense attorney was ineffective (later disbarred)

## What CRT Should Ignore
- Weather reports, school enrollment, vehicle descriptions
- Unlinked sightings, anonymous tips, inconclusive forensics

## Ground Truth
- Conviction vacated September 2022
- DNA testing excluded Syed
- Charges dropped October 2022
- Conviction later reinstated on procedural grounds (victim notification)
- March 2025: Sentence reduced to time served + probation
